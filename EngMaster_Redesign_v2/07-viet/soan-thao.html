Nowadays, most teenagers spend severals hours a day on social media platforms such as TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

In my opinion, these apps are not bad in themselves. They help young people keep in touch with friends and discover new ideas. However, many students find it difficult to concentrate on their homework becuase notifications never stop.

I beleive the solution is not to ban social media, but to teach teenagers how to use it with intention.
