Bài mẫu

Đề luyện tập, viết đúng theo 13 câu của outline trên (số trên mỗi câu là số câu trong outline):

Some people think that advertisements for unhealthy food and drinks should be banned. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

1It is sometimes believed that a ban should be imposed on advertisements for unhealthy food and drinks. 2This essay totally agrees with this suggestion for several reasons.

3The first argument given to support my opinion is that such advertisements encourage poor eating habits, especially among children. 4This is because these adverts are designed to make sugary snacks and fast food look fun and desirable, so young viewers often cannot resist them. 5As a result, children tend to pester their parents for these products, and unhealthy food gradually becomes part of their daily diet. 6For example, many fast-food chains offer free toys with children's meals and promote them heavily on television, which makes these meals extremely popular with young children. 7This is why I believe that banning such advertisements would go a long way towards protecting children's health.

8Another point behind my belief is that a ban would reduce the burden on public healthcare systems. 9This is mainly because diets high in sugar and fat are closely linked to obesity, diabetes and heart disease. 10Therefore, if fewer people consumed these products, governments would spend less money treating such illnesses and could invest more in education or infrastructure. 11To illustrate, a patient with type 2 diabetes may need medication and regular check-ups for the rest of their life, all of which is expensive for the state. 12For these reasons, I think that restricting these advertisements would benefit not only individuals but also society as a whole.

13In conclusion, I totally agree that unhealthy food and drinks should no longer be advertised given the aforementioned arguments.

  • Câu 1 dùng cụm ghi chú tay a ban … imposed on để paraphrase should be banned.
  • Câu 4, 9 giải thích; câu 5, 10 nói hệ quả; câu 6, 11 là ví dụ; câu 7, 12 dùng hai câu Link viết tay.
  • Bài dài 263 từ, đạt yêu cầu tối thiểu 250 từ.
